ITENFRDE

1–4 October 2026 · Massagno

Program

Four days across Cinema Lux, Sala Cosmo, the Massagno schools and the piazza. Competition screenings and out-of-competition screenings are ticketed; everything else is free to walk into.

Thu 1 Fri 2 Sat 3 Sun 4 Mon 5

Tickets and entry

Competition screenings, out-of-competition screenings and the opening and closing ceremonies are ticketed: CHF 15.– for a single screening, CHF 30.– for a Friday or Saturday day pass. Students and over-65s pay CHF 10.– and CHF 20.–. Everything else in the programme is free. With an accreditation your seat costs nothing — one per screening.
