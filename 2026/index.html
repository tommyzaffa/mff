The first edition

1–4 October 2026 at Cinema Lux, Massagno (Lugano). Four days of cinema and artificial intelligence: this is how the first Merge Film Festival went.

In numbers Winners Audience vote Films Juries Programme

In numbers

The first edition in numbers

Switzerland’s first film festival dedicated to artificial intelligence opened with three competitions, eight awards and a room full of students.

  • ≈ 370films submitted from around the world
  • 36films in competition, across three sections
  • 17countries and territories on screen
  • 8awards, plus three honourable mentions
  • 867audience votes, cast by 107 viewers
  • 180accreditations for guests, industry, press and staff
  • ≈ 150viewers per screening, on average
  • 273seats, every one taken for WALL·E with the schools

Audience Award

The audience vote

The audience rated every film from 1 to 10 on their phones, from the start of each competition screening until three hours after it ended: one vote per film per device.

  • 867votes cast
  • 107viewers who voted
  • 36films rated, all of them

The audience’s top five

Programme

Four days at the Lux

From Thursday evening to Sunday afternoon, between Cinema Lux and the schools of Massagno.

  • 5competition programmes, each with a Q&A with the directors
  • 3films out of competition
  • 2masterclasses for high schools
  • 2roundtables
  • 1panel on the environmental impact of AI
  • 1conference
  • 1open talk: the launch of FORMA APERTA
  • 1screening & conversation with Alessandro Bavari
  • 2ceremonies, to open and to close

Thursday 1 October

  • Opening ceremony
  • Opening film: Memory of Princess Mumbi by Damien Hauser

Friday 2 October

  • Masterclasses for high schools with Antonella Autuori and Stefano Peduzzi (SUPSI)
  • Workshops for middle schools with SUPSI
  • WALL·E out of competition, to a full house of students
  • Competition programmes 1 and 2
  • Open talk and launch of FORMA APERTA
  • Screening & conversation with Alessandro Bavari
  • Swiss Innovator Award and Swiss Press Award

Saturday 3 October

  • Wider than the Sky by Valerio Jalongo, out of competition
  • Roundtable: How is the image changing?
  • Roundtable on work and the role of human beings in the age of AI, with USI
  • Panel on the environmental impact of AI
  • Conference with Javishth Chabria and Kevin B. Lee (USI)
  • Competition programmes 3, 4 and 5
  • Critics’ Award by Filmexplorer

Sunday 4 October

  • Awards ceremony
  • Screening of the winning films
